How to read this demo

Illustrations, not the statistical test

The displayed carrier for each feature was selected after analysis because it makes the intended effect especially visible. The statistical summaries use all available carrier prompts and paired random controls. All examples except the sawtooth-program intervention remain exploratory; the four qualitative candidates come from a blinded piano-roll review whose aggregate result was null. Piano rolls are coloured by MIDI program. Audio was synthesized from the generated MIDI and independently peak-normalized.
